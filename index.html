# Privacy Policy for NUR **Last updated: September 21, 2026** NUR respects your privacy and is designed to work without requiring users to create an account. ## Information We Collect NUR does not require registration and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or account credentials. ## Local Data Certain information, such as application settings, bookmarks, reading progress, Khatm progress, and other preferences, may be stored locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the application's functionality and is not sent to NUR servers. Users can remove locally stored application data by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application. ## Advertising NUR does not display advertisements and does not use advertising services for user tracking. ## Third-Party Services The application may rely on standard services provided by the Android operating system or Google Play for application distribution, security, diagnostics, and other platform functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies. ## Children's Privacy NUR does not knowingly collect personal information from children. ## Data Security Because NUR does not require an account and application-related user data is stored locally on the user's device, NUR does not maintain a database of user accounts or personal profiles. ## Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data practices change. Any changes will be reflected on this page. ## Contact If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer using the contact information provided on the NUR Google Play listing.
